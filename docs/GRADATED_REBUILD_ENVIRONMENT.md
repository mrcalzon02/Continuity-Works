# Gradated Rebuild Environment

The system chooses the **minimum sufficient intervention** under a caller-specified ceiling.

## Grade 0 — AUDIT_ONLY
No mutation. Inventory geometry, integration, provenance, context, function, access, clearance, visual issues and missing evidence.

## Grade 1 — TOUCH_UP
Compatibility and obvious defects only:
- broken states/IDs;
- tiny holes;
- accidental blockers;
- missing door/ladder connection;
- minor terrain contact;
- minimal palette correction.

Do not redesign massing or purpose.

## Grade 2 — REFIT
Local architectural/operational corrections:
- entrance hierarchy;
- corridor/door clearances;
- localized room use;
- fixtures/utilities;
- coherent damage repair;
- small facade depth improvements.

## Grade 3 — DETAIL_PASS
Increase professional readability without replacing the building:
- facade rhythm/depth;
- roof systems;
- utility/service spaces;
- interiors by room purpose;
- landscaping/site infrastructure;
- controlled cultural language.

## Grade 4 — FUNCTIONAL_REBUILD
Rework circulation and program when the existing shell fails its purpose:
- reorganize rooms;
- move stairs/doors;
- repair operational sequences;
- add required service zones;
- adjust limited massing.

Major identity remains.

## Grade 5 — HEAVY_REBUILD
Treat rough geometry as a sketch:
- derive real institutional program;
- precedents;
- new massing as necessary;
- professional circulation and structure;
- utilities/site systems;
- cultural/institutional architecture;
- history/damage/encounter integration;
- multiple visual gates.

Preserve authoritative IDs and external integration unless explicitly corrected.

## Grade 6 — FULL_RECONTEXTUALIZATION
For assets whose footprint/site relationship/purpose is fundamentally wrong. Re-author geometry and site interaction while retaining declared contracts. This is not permission to erase provenance or required integration.

## Escalation rule

Escalate only when a lower grade cannot pass the failed gate. Record the reason in the snapshot.
