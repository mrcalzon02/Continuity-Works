# Clearance and Access Levels

Two independent clearance systems are used.

## Physical clearance

- `micro`: decorative/mechanical microspace.
- `person`: minimum individual traversal.
- `public_circulation`: broad institutional/public traversal.
- `cart`: trolley/minecart/service cart.
- `vehicle`: truck/car/forklift-like route.
- `submersible`: underwater vehicle approach/entry corridor.
- `industrial`: major machinery/service route.
- `megastructure`: unusually large moving equipment or architectural opening.

Clearance should be checked through the full route, not just at one doorway.

## Access/security clearance

- `public`
- `staff`
- `controlled`
- `restricted`
- `secure`
- `blacksite`

This affects route hierarchy, barriers, vestibules, checkpoints, evidence/loot placement and encounter staging. It must not be confused with physical passage size.
