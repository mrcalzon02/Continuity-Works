- Lower Paleolithic fire-management ceiling and explicit prohibited later-era technologies;
- S/M/L fire-focus, clearance, behavioral-footprint, and occupancy classes;

- wind/smoke vector derivation and cave-airflow substitution;

- heat/ember/spark/warmth/safe-occupation distance-band logic;
- deterministic angular activity-sector generation rather than uniform decorative rings;
- repeated-use and extinguished-state history passes;
- biome/environment adaptations including cave ventilation constraints;
- culture-variant hooks bounded by era technology;
- local substrate, loose stone, fuel, residue, and prohibited-material palette logic;
- active, extinguished, repeated, neglected, weather-damaged, abandoned, archaeological, and later-repurposed condition states;
- jigsaw/family relationships with shelters, work areas, and processing sites while preserving distinction from E01-008 Multi-Hearth Gathering Site;
- environmental/resource infrastructure dependencies;
- distributed trace-style loot and sector-aware occupancy hooks;
- additive/non-destructive compatibility rules;
- default minimum 500-block unrelated-structure exclusion rule;
- geometry/fire-safety, smoke/environment, archetype, historical, procedural, compatibility, and visual validation criteria;
