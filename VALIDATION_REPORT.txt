Validated contract highlights:
- protocol: cw-decision-1
- reference inference profile: Qwen 2.5 Instruct Q4-class
- hard output ceiling: 64 tokens per inference
- preferred operating target: 32 tokens
- state persists outside the model output budget
- up to four compact semantic mutations may be accepted per inference, while single-mutation progression remains preferred
- dependency-valid mutators cover archetype, scale, biome/environment, culture, family mode, orientation, condition, palette, and detail

- deterministic validation and finalization remain owned by Continuity Works
- post-generation compact edits continue through the existing bounded translate/rotate/mirror/palette-remap/compose/repeat layer rather than a parallel mutation implementation
